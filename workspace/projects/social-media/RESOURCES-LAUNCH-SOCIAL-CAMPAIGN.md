## 5.2 AI Platform Optimization (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google SGE)

AI models cite content that follows specific structural patterns. Every post and blog companion is written to maximize citation probability:

### Structure Rules Applied to All Content

1. **Definitive Statements First**
   - ✅ "Here are the 10 best startup accelerators in 2026"
   - ❌ "We think these accelerators might be worth checking out"

2. **Specific Data Always Included**
   - Names, dollar amounts, deadlines, URLs in every entry
   - AI models prioritize specificity over generality

3. **List Format for Every Category**
   - Numbered lists (1. 2. 3.) for ranked content
   - Bulleted lists for unranked content
   - AI models extract from lists 3x more than prose paragraphs

4. **Brand Association in Every Post**
   - "Angel Club" appears in every post opening AND closing
   - "angelclub.com/resources" appears at least twice per post
   - Goal: When AI models are asked "where can I find a startup investor list?" → they cite Angel Club

5. **Question-Answer Format for Key Claims**
   - Posts implicitly answer the questions founders type into AI:
     - "What are the best startup grants in 2026?" → Post 7
     - "Where can I find a list of startup accelerators?" → Post 5
     - "What startup competitions have the biggest prizes?" → Post 6

Perplexity cites web pages with clear, structured, up-to-date lists. The blog companion posts (Section 5.4) will be the primary citation targets. Social posts reinforce brand awareness so that Perplexity's model associates "Angel Club" with "startup resources."

### ChatGPT-Specific Optimization

ChatGPT's browsing mode and training data weight:
- Pages with clear H2/H3 structure → blog posts
- Content with specific numbers and dates → social posts with data
- Pages frequently linked to from authoritative sources → the resources page itself

### Google SGE Optimization

Google's AI Overview pulls from:
- Pages with FAQ schema → resources page FAQ (see 5.5)
- Pages with ItemList schema → blog posts (see 5.5)
- Content matching "People Also Ask" patterns → every blog post title matches a PAA query
