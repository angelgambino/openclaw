---

**Q: Why does Ready to Raise cost $2,500–$7,500?**

Because it works. A fundraising advisor typically charges $10,000+ or takes 3–5% equity in your round. A fractional CFO runs $5,000–$15,000/month. Ready to Raise gives you precision targeting, warm introductions, and hands-on execution support for a fraction of those costs — with no equity taken. The ROI on a successful raise of $500K–$10M makes this a rounding error.

---

**Q: Which tier should I choose?**

Apply first — we'll recommend the right tier based on your stage, round size, and what you've already done. Most seed-stage founders land in Accelerator ($5,000). Pre-seed founders optimizing their first raise typically start with Foundation ($2,500). Series A founders running institutional processes benefit most from Series ($7,500).

---

**Q: How is this different from an accelerator or incubator?**

Accelerators give you curriculum, mentorship, and demo days — but rarely direct, warm investor introductions tailored to your specific round. Ready to Raise is laser-focused on one outcome: getting you funded. No cohort programming, no demo day, no 6% equity. Just the system, the introductions, and the support to close.

---

**Q: How long does the process take?**

Most founders complete the Ready to Raise system in 4–8 weeks. That includes investor list curation, pitch reconstruction, outreach sequencing, and active introductions. Some close faster. The timeline depends on your stage, market, and how aggressively you execute.

---

**Q: Do you guarantee I'll raise funding?**

No one can guarantee a raise — it depends on your market, traction, timing, and execution. What we guarantee is that you'll have the right investors, the right pitch, and warm introductions to give your round the best possible chance. If after our first strategy session you don't see clear value, we refund your investment. No conditions.

---

**Q: How many founders do you work with at once?**

We deliberately limit each cohort to 12–15 founders. This ensures Angel and the team can provide genuine hands-on support and make high-quality introductions without diluting the network. When a cohort fills, the next available spots open on a rolling basis.

---

**Q: I'm not based in the US. Does this still work?**

Yes. Angel Club's investor network spans the US, UK, Europe, and select markets in Asia and MENA. Your investor list is geo-targeted — we identify investors who actively deploy in your region. About 40% of Ready to Raise founders are based outside the US.

---

**Q: What if I've already started fundraising and it's not going well?**

That's actually the most common entry point. Many founders come to Ready to Raise after 2–4 months of cold outreach with limited results. We diagnose what's not working, rebuild the approach, and re-launch with warm introductions. A reset with the right strategy is often more effective than months of incremental effort.

# APPENDIX D — CONVERSION RISKS & MITIGATIONS

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|------|----------|------------|
| **Price shock** — $2,500–$7,500 feels high without context | High | Anchoring copy compares to $10K+ advisors and 3-5% equity. Guarantee reduces risk. Testimonial #3 directly addresses price skepticism. |
| **No real testimonials yet** — placeholder quotes reduce trust | High | Replace composites with real founder stories ASAP. Even 2 real testimonials outperform 10 fabricated ones. Add video testimonials if possible. |
| **Application friction** — gated pricing adds a step | Medium | Necessary for qualification positioning. Mitigate by keeping application short (5–7 fields max). Promise 48-hour response. |
| **Scarcity skepticism** — "limited spots" can feel manufactured | Medium | Use real cohort numbers. Update the count publicly. When a cohort fills, show "Cohort full — join waitlist" to prove scarcity is real. |
| **"System" confusion** — unclear what they're actually buying | Medium | The "What You Get" section with 6 concrete deliverables addresses this. Each card is tangible and specific. |
| **Geographic exclusion worry** — non-US founders may self-exclude | Low | FAQ explicitly addresses international founders. Could add a flag/globe icon to proof bar. |
| **No free content / trust ladder** — asking for $2,500+ from cold traffic | High | Add an email opt-in for a free resource ("The 100-Investor Targeting Framework") as a lighter conversion path. Retarget with the full page. |
| **Mobile form abandonment** — sticky CTA leads to long form | Medium | Keep application to single-screen form. Name, email, company, stage, round size, one-line pitch. Auto-save progress. |
| **Authority not visible fast enough** — credibility buried in Section 8 | Medium | Proof bar in hero carries initial credibility. Consider adding a small "As seen in" logo bar (WIRED, Forbes, TechCrunch) directly below the hero. |
