## 1. SSI APPLICATION (Do This FIRST)

### What You Need:
- Bronco's Social Security number
- Birth certificate or proof of citizenship
- ASD diagnosis documentation (from licensed psychologist/psychiatrist)
- ADHD diagnosis documentation
- Medical records (all providers, therapists, psychiatrists)
- Functional limitations report — HOW ASD/ADHD affects daily living (communication, social interaction, self-care, concentration, following instructions)
- Proof of income (should be $0 or minimal at 18)
- Proof of resources (bank accounts — must be under $2,000)
- Living arrangement info

### How to Apply:
1. **Online:** ssa.gov/applyfordisability
2. **Phone:** 1-800-772-1213 (TTY 1-800-325-0778)
3. **In-person:** 
   - **San Rafael SSA Office:** 30 N. San Pedro Rd, Suite 250, San Rafael, CA 94903 | (866) 964-4360
   - Hours: Mon-Fri 9am-4pm, walk-in or appointment

### Tips for Approval:
- **Emphasize FUNCTIONAL LIMITATIONS, not just the diagnosis.** SSA doesn't care that he has ASD — they care about what he CAN'T do. Document:
  - Difficulty with social interactions
  - Sensory processing issues
  - Executive function challenges (ADHD)
  - Need for routine/structure
  - Difficulty with self-care, cooking, transportation
  - Employment barriers
- **Get a detailed letter from his diagnosing doctor** specifically describing how ASD+ADHD limit his ability to work
- **Don't minimize.** Describe his WORST days, not his best
- **File even if you think he might be denied.** The application date locks in potential back-pay

### Timeline:
- Initial decision: 3-6 months
- If denied → Reconsideration: 1-3 months
- If denied again → ALJ hearing: 6-18 months (this is where a lawyer matters)
- **Approval rate:** ~30% initially, ~65% at ALJ hearing with attorney

## 2. GOLDEN GATE REGIONAL CENTER (GGRC)

### Contact:
- **Website:** ggrc.org
- **Phone:** (415) 546-9222
- **Main Office:** 1355 Market St, Suite 220, San Francisco, CA 94103
- **Intake line:** Call and say: "I'd like to request an intake assessment for my 18-year-old son who has an ASD diagnosis."

### Services for Adults with ASD (18+):
- **Supported Employment** — job coaching, job placement, workplace support
- **Independent Living Skills** — cooking, money management, transportation, self-care
- **Day Programs** — structured activities, socialization, community integration
- **Behavioral Therapy** — ABA or other behavioral support
- **Respite Care** — gives you a break, pays someone to stay with Bronco
- **Transportation** — help getting to appointments, programs, work
- **Self-Determination Program** — YOU control the budget for his services
- **Transition Planning** — specifically for 18+ entering adulthood
- **Camp/Recreation** — social activities, community programs

### How to Apply:
1. Call (415) 546-9222 and request intake
2. They'll schedule an assessment (usually within 30-60 days)
3. Bring: ASD diagnosis, medical records, IEP if he had one in school
4. They assign a Service Coordinator who becomes your ongoing contact
5. Together you create an Individual Program Plan (IPP) — your roadmap for services
6. **Services are FREE** — funded by California Department of Developmental Services

### Important: 
- **You do NOT need SSI approval to get Regional Center services.** Apply to both simultaneously.
- Regional Center is a LIFETIME entitlement in California for people with developmental disabilities

## 3. IHSS (IN-HOME SUPPORTIVE SERVICES)

### This is the big one for immediate income.

IHSS pays a caregiver — **which can be YOU, Angel** — to help Bronco with daily tasks.

### How to Apply:
- **Marin County IHSS:** (415) 473-2903
- **Online:** Apply through BenefitsCal.com
- **In-person:** Marin County Health & Human Services, 10 N. San Pedro Rd, San Rafael, CA 94903
- Can apply as soon as SSI is approved (or apply for both simultaneously)

### What It Covers:
- Meal preparation
- Housework/laundry
- Transportation to appointments
- Paramedical services (medication reminders)
- Protective supervision (for safety — this is the highest-hour category for ASD)
- Personal care assistance

### Pay Rates (Marin County 2026):
- **$18.50-20.50/hour** (Marin is above state minimum)
- **Maximum hours:** Up to 283 hours/month for highest-need cases
- **Protective supervision** alone can be 195+ hours/month for ASD

### What This Means:
- At 195 hours protective supervision × $19/hr = **$3,705/month**
- Plus other task hours = potentially **$4,000-5,660/month**
- **This is paid to YOU as his caregiver** — it's W-2 income

### How It Works:
1. Apply for IHSS
2. County sends a social worker to assess Bronco's needs (in-home visit)
3. They authorize a certain number of hours per month per task
4. You register as his IHSS provider (simple form)
5. You log hours on the IHSS timesheet system
6. You get paid every 2 weeks via direct deposit

## 4. MEDI-CAL

### Automatic with SSI — no separate application needed.

Once SSI is approved, Medi-Cal kicks in automatically.

### What It Covers for ASD/ADHD:
- Behavioral health therapy (ABA, CBT)
- Psychiatry + medication management
- Occupational therapy
- Speech therapy (if needed)
- Mental health services
- Primary care
- Dental (Denti-Cal)
- Vision
- Prescriptions (including ADHD medication)

### If SSI Takes a While:
- Apply for Medi-Cal separately through CoveredCA.com or BenefitsCal.com
- At 18 with no income, Bronco likely qualifies for Medi-Cal expansion immediately

## 5. CalABLE ACCOUNT

### What It Is:
Tax-advantaged savings account for people with disabilities. Savings DON'T count against the $2,000 SSI resource limit.

### How to Open:
- **Website:** CalABLE.ca.gov
- Can save up to **$100,000** without affecting SSI eligibility
- Up to **$18,000/year** in contributions
- Money can be used for housing, education, transportation, health, assistive technology, job training
- Tax-free growth

### Open this AFTER SSI is approved to protect any savings.
