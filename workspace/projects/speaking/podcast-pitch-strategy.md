## Pitch Best Practices

1. **Personalize every pitch** — Reference a specific recent episode and why your topic extends/complements it
2. **Lead with what's in it for THEIR audience** — Not your bio
3. **One clear topic per pitch** — Don't list 8 things you could talk about
4. **Include social proof concisely** — WIRED Top 100, TEDx, 5 exits, 40+ investments (pick 2-3)
5. **Keep it under 150 words** — Producers read hundreds of pitches
6. **Follow up once at 7 days, once at 14** — Then move on
7. **Pitch on Tuesday-Thursday** — Avoid Monday inbox overwhelm and Friday wind-down

## Leveraging One Appearance into More

1. **Create clips immediately** — Pull 3-5 short clips (60-90 sec) for social media within 24 hours
2. **Tag the host and show** — On LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and Instagram
3. **Write a LinkedIn post** expanding on one point from the interview
4. **Cross-reference in future pitches** — "As I recently discussed on [Show Name]..."
5. **Build a podcast appearances page** on your website
6. **Send thank-you + share metrics** — "Hey [Host], my post about our episode got 50K impressions — thanks again!"
7. **Ask for introductions** — After a great episode, ask the host "Who else should I talk to?"

## Social Media Strategy Around Appearances

### Pre-Appearance
- Announce the recording (creates anticipation)
- Ask your community what questions they'd want asked

### Day of Release
- Share on all platforms with unique commentary per platform
- LinkedIn: Longer reflection post + episode link
- Twitter/X: Thread with 3-5 key takeaways + clip
- Instagram: Audiogram or video clip in Stories + Reels

### Post-Appearance (Week After)
- Share individual clips throughout the week (don't dump all at once)
- Engage with every comment on the episode
- Connect with other guests from the show

## Warm Intro Strategy

For Tier A shows especially, cold pitches have low conversion. Prioritize:

1. **Check Angel Club network** — Do any of the 6,400+ founders or 400+ investors know these hosts?
2. **Check LinkedIn mutual connections** — Ask for warm intros
3. **Engage on social first** — Comment thoughtfully on host's content for 2-4 weeks before pitching
4. **Attend live events** — Many podcast hosts do live shows or speak at conferences
5. **Guest on smaller shows first** — Build a reel, then pitch up
