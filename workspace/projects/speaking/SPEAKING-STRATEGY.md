### Pricing Strategy

**Phase 1: Establish (Months 1-6)**
- Starting fee: $10K-$15K for corporate, $7.5K-$10K for associations
- Accept 2-3 strategic "low fee" gigs ($5K or free) for video footage + testimonials
- Target: 5-10 paid talks

**Phase 2: Scale (Months 7-18)**
- Raise to $15K-$25K as demand builds
- Use testimonials + video from Phase 1 to justify increases
- Bureau representation amplifies pricing
- Target: 15-25 paid talks/year

**Phase 3: Premium (Months 18-36)**
- Raise to $25K-$50K
- Published book accelerates this timeline
- Bureau handles pricing conversations
- Target: 20-30 paid talks/year at $25K+ average

**Phase 4: Elite (Year 3+)**
- $50K-$100K+ per keynote
- Requires: bestselling book, viral talk, or massive media moment
- Only 2-3% of speakers reach this level
- Target: 20+ talks/year at $50K+ average

### Professional Headshots Needed
- **Hero shot:** Full-body or 3/4, on stage or dynamic setting (for one-sheet and website hero)
- **Corporate headshot:** Head/shoulders, neutral background, professional (for bio/speaker pages)
- **Casual/authentic shot:** Less formal, approachable (for social media and "meet the speaker" features)
- **On-stage action shots:** Speaking at TEDx or other events (for sizzle reel and social proof)

**Budget:** $1,000-$3,000 for professional shoot. Worth every penny.

### Sizzle Reel Requirements
- **Length:** 2-3 minutes max
- **Content:**

  - Brief testimonial clips if available
  - Closing with contact/booking info
- **Production quality:** Professional editing, good audio, lower thirds
- **Budget:** $2,000-$5,000 for professional editing

**Pro tip:** Also create 30-60 second "snackable" clips for social media from the same footage.

## 3. OUTREACH STRATEGY

### How to Approach Speaker Agencies

**What agencies want to see before signing you:**
1. ✅ Professional sizzle reel (2-3 minutes)
2. ✅ Full-length video of at least one complete talk (TEDx counts!)
3. ✅ Speaker one-sheet
4. ✅ Evidence of past paid bookings (even at lower fees)
5. ✅ Clear, differentiated topic positioning
6. ✅ Strong social media presence (especially LinkedIn)
7. ✅ Media mentions / press clips
8. ✅ Testimonials from past event organizers

**Approach sequence:**
1. **Start with Tier C** platforms (eSpeakers, SpeakerHub, Sessionize) — get listed, get discovered
2. **Then Tier B** agencies (BigSpeak, Eagles Talent, Lavin Agency) — they're more accessible and actively recruit
3. **Then Tier A** (after building a track record of 15-20 paid talks + strong reel) — WSB, Keppler, Leading Authorities
4. **Note:** You can work with multiple Tier B/C agencies simultaneously. Tier A agencies typically want exclusivity.

### Leveraging Angel Club Network

Angel Club = 6,800+ founders and investors. This is a MASSIVE speaking lead engine:

1. **Post in Angel Club:** "I'm expanding my speaking practice. If your company, portfolio company, or industry event is looking for a keynote speaker on [topics], I'd love to chat."
2. **Ask for referrals:** "Know an event organizer or conference programmer who should hear about my speaking? Intro = coffee on me."
3. **Offer value first:** Speak at Angel Club events, record it, use the footage
4. **Leverage investors in the club:** VC/angel investors attend conferences, sit on boards of companies that host events, and know event organizers
5. **Create a "Speaker Ambassador" program:** Offer Angel Club members a referral fee ($500-$1,000) for confirmed paid bookings

### Referral Strategy

The #1 way speakers get booked is referrals from past event organizers.

1. After every talk, ask the organizer: "Do you know 2-3 other event organizers who might be interested in this topic for their audience?"
2. Ask for a warm email intro (much more effective than cold outreach)
3. Send a thank-you gift after every referral (book, wine, handwritten note)
4. Keep a referral tracker — who referred whom, result, follow-up date
5. **Goal:** Every talk should generate 2-3 referral leads

## 4. SOCIAL MEDIA FOR SPEAKERS

### What to Post to Attract Speaking Bookers

Event planners and bookers scroll social media to find speakers. Every post should subtly signal "I'm a speaker worth booking."

**Content mix (weekly):**
- 🎤 **1x On-stage/speaking content:** Photo from a talk, video clip, behind-the-scenes
- 💡 **2x Thought leadership:** Insights on your speaking topics (AI, entrepreneurship, investing)
- 📊 **1x Data/insight:** Share an interesting stat or trend with your take
- 🗣️ **1x Testimonial/social proof:** Quote from an event organizer, attendee feedback, audience photo
- 🎬 **1x Video clip:** 30-60 second clip from a past talk (the MOST powerful content type for speakers)

### Platform-Specific Strategy

**LinkedIn (PRIMARY — where bookers live):**
- Post 3-4x/week
- Mix of text posts, video clips, and images
- Use Creator Mode for larger reach
- Join and engage in event planning groups
- Comment thoughtfully on posts by other speakers and event organizers
- **The goal:** When an event planner searches "AI speaker" or "entrepreneurship keynote," your name comes up

**Instagram:**
- Post 2-3x/week to feed, 3-5x/week to stories
- On-stage photos, behind-the-scenes, sizzle reel clips
- Reels of speaking clips perform exceptionally well
- Use location tags at events
- Hashtags: #KeynoteSpeaker #ConferenceSpeaker #WomenInTech #AILeadership

**Twitter/X:**
- Share insights from your speaking topics
- Live-tweet from events you attend
- Engage with conference accounts (@SXSW, @WebSummit, etc.)
- Quote-tweet event announcements with your perspective
- **Thread format:** Share "5 things I learned from [topic]" threads

### Content That Signals "Book Me" Without Saying It

The best speaker marketing never says "hire me to speak." Instead:

✅ **Do post:**
- "Just wrapped an incredible session at [event]. The audience questions about AI + human potential were 🔥"
- "The #1 question I get when I'm on stage: [question]. Here's my answer..."
- "Behind the scenes at [event] — love connecting with fellow founders after the keynote"
- Video clip: "Here's what I told 500 CEOs about why the best founders break every rule..."
- "Grateful for this feedback from [organizer]: '[testimonial quote]'"

❌ **Don't post:**
- "Hire me to speak at your event!"
- "Available for bookings in Q3"
- "DM me for my speaking rate"
- (Too direct = desperation signal)

### How to Use Video Clips from Past Talks

Video is the #1 driver of speaking bookings. Event planners want to SEE you on stage.

1. **Clip every talk:** Even if just phone recordings initially
2. **Create 30-60 second clips:** Best moment, funniest line, most impactful insight
3. **Post clips weekly** on LinkedIn, IG Reels, Twitter
4. **Caption with context:** "From my talk at [event] about [topic]..."
5. **Longer clips (3-5 min)** for YouTube and website
6. **Full talks** on YouTube (gated or ungated — opinions differ; recommendation: post at least one full talk publicly)

## 5. REVENUE MODEL

### Path to $500K/Year
- **20 keynotes × $25K average = $500K**
- Timeline: 18-24 months
- Mix: 10 corporate ($30K avg) + 5 conferences ($20K avg) + 5 association/other ($15K avg)
- Add: 5-10 Vistage sessions at $5K = $25K-$50K bonus

### Path to $1M/Year
**Option A:** 20 keynotes × $50K average = $1M
- Requires: bestselling book, viral talk, or major media moment
- Timeline: 3-5 years

**Option B:** 40 keynotes × $25K average = $1M
- More realistic near-term
- Requires: efficient logistics, potentially multiple talks per trip
- Timeline: 2-3 years

**Option C (Recommended):** Blended model
- 15 premium keynotes × $35K = $525K
- 10 corporate workshops × $15K = $150K
- 20 Vistage/EO/YPO sessions × $7.5K = $150K
- Virtual talks + course licensing = $100K
- Consulting add-on from speaking leads = $75K-$100K
- **Total: ~$1M**
- Timeline: 2-3 years

### Path to $5M-$10M (The Big Play)
At this level, speaking is the front door, not the whole house:

| Revenue Stream | Annual Revenue | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Keynotes (25 × $75K avg) | $1.8M | Requires bestseller or massive platform |
| Corporate workshops/training | $1M | Half-day or full-day programs at $25K-$50K each |
| Online course/masterclass | $500K-$1M | Recorded once, sold repeatedly |
| Corporate licensing (talk + workshop IP) | $500K-$1M | License your methodology to companies |
| Consulting (sourced from speaking) | $500K-$1M | Every keynote = 2-3 consulting leads |
| Book royalties + advances | $200K-$500K | Bestselling business book |
| Executive coaching | $200K-$500K | Premium 1:1 coaching for executives |
| **TOTAL** | **$4.7M-$6.8M** | |

### How Top Speakers Structure Revenue

**Brené Brown model ($10M+):**
- Books (5+ bestsellers) drive speaking demand
- Speaking at $200K+ per keynote
- Netflix special (massive reach)
- Corporate training program (Dare to Lead™) licensed to hundreds of companies
- Podcast (Unlocking Us) drives ongoing visibility

**Simon Sinek model ($10M+):**
- Books (Start With Why, etc.) as foundation
- Speaking at $100K+ per keynote
- "The Optimism Company" — consulting arm
- Online courses and video content
- Brand partnerships and IP licensing

**Key pattern:** The highest-earning speakers ALL have:
1. A bestselling book (usually multiple)
2. A signature framework/methodology
3. IP that can be licensed (workshops, training programs)
4. Multiple content formats (talks + books + courses + podcasts)
5. A team (agent, manager, content team, business manager)

### Ancillary Revenue Streams

1. **Corporate workshops ($15K-$50K each):** Deep-dive sessions after keynotes. "Loved the keynote? Book a half-day workshop for your leadership team."
2. **Masterclasses ($5K-$15K per session):** Smaller, intimate sessions for executive groups
3. **Consulting add-on:** Every keynote introduces you to potential consulting clients. "After hearing your talk, we'd love to have you advise our team on [topic]."
4. **Online courses:** Record a signature program. Sell directly ($500-$2,000) or license to companies ($10K-$50K per license)
5. **VIP dinners:** Post-keynote dinner with 10-15 executives. Add $5K-$10K to the keynote fee.
6. **Podcast:** Free to produce, drives speaking demand. "Heard Angel on her podcast → booked her for our event."
