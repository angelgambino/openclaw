# Hubble Call Follow-Up Sequence

**Purpose:** Convert Hubble call participants into either **Ready to Raise** (paid program) or **a second Hubble call** (relationship-building). Every email offers both paths. A second Hubble call is a win — it keeps them in the funnel.

**Tone:** Warm, genuine, not salesy. Angel is a peer who's been through it, not a closer.

**Personalization notes:** Replace all `[bracketed]` items with specifics from the Hubble call. The more specific, the better.

**Dual-CTA philosophy:** Every email should make R2R feel like the obvious next step for founders who are ready, while making a second Hubble call feel like a natural, zero-pressure option for everyone else. Never make someone feel bad for not being ready for R2R.

## Post-Second-Hubble Sequence

**If a founder does a second Hubble call and still doesn't convert to R2R, here's what happens next:**

>
> **🎪 Angel Club events** — I'm adding you to our invite list for upcoming dinners and roundtables. You'll get an invite when we have something relevant. No commitment, just show up if it works.
>
> **📬 The newsletter** — We send a monthly roundup of fundraising insights, investor activity, and founder wins. I'll add you unless you'd rather not — just say the word.
>
> Keep building. I'll be here when you need me.
>
> — Angel

### Ongoing Nurture (post-second Hubble, no R2R conversion)

**Move them to the Angel Club ecosystem. The relationship continues — just through different channels:**

| Timeframe | Action | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Immediately** | Add to Angel Club event invite list | Keep them in the orbit |
| **Immediately** | Add to Angel Club newsletter | Monthly touchpoint with value |
| **30 days** | Invite to next Jeffersonian dinner or roundtable | In-person relationship building |
| **60 days** | Personal check-in (email or DM) | "Hey, how's the raise going?" — genuine, not salesy |
| **90 days** | Re-offer Hubble call | "A lot can change in 3 months — want to hop on another call?" |
| **Ongoing** | React to their content on social | Stay visible without being pushy |
| **When relevant** | Make warm intros | Connect them with investors or founders — build goodwill |

### Re-Entry Triggers

**Bring them back into the R2R conversation when:**
- They mention they're actively raising (on social, in a group chat, at an event)
- They attend an Angel Club event and express frustration with their raise
- They reach out proactively with a question about fundraising
- A mutual connection mentions they're struggling with their raise
- They engage heavily with fundraising content in the newsletter

**How to re-engage:**
> Hey [Name] — I saw [trigger]. I remember from our Hubble calls that [callback to specific challenge]. Has that shifted? If you're in active raise mode, Ready to Raise might be the right move now. Or happy to do a quick call to see where things stand. [R2R Link] / [Hubble Link]

### The Golden Rule

**A founder who does two Hubble calls and doesn't convert to R2R is not a lost lead — they're a community member.** They've invested 40+ minutes of conversation with Angel. They trust him. They'll either:
1. Convert to R2R later when the timing is right
2. Refer other founders to Hubble/R2R
3. Become Angel Club community members who attend events and add value
4. Raise successfully on their own and become part of the network as alumni

All four of these are wins. Treat them accordingly.

## When to Stop Following Up

**Post-first Hubble (if they don't respond to any emails):**
- Stop after Email 5 (Day 21)
- Move to Angel Club general list for event invites and newsletter
- No more direct R2R outreach unless they re-engage

**Post-second Hubble:**
- Move immediately to the nurture track above
- No direct R2R outreach for at least 90 days
- Focus on community value, not conversion

**Hard stop rules:**
- ❌ Never send more than 5 emails without a reply (per sequence)
- ❌ If they unsubscribe or ask to stop, stop immediately
- ❌ Don't follow up on weekends or holidays
- ❌ Don't send more than 1 email per week in the active sequence
- ❌ After 2 Hubble calls + no R2R conversion, no more than 1 personal outreach per quarter

**Sequence automation notes:**
- Tag each contact with: Hubble call dates, key topics, number of calls completed, R2R interest level (hot/warm/cool)
- Set reminders for each email send date
- Track opens/replies to adjust timing (if someone opens Email 3 multiple times, consider moving up Email 4)
- If they reply at any point, exit the sequence and have a real conversation
- If they book a second Hubble call at any point in the sequence, exit the sequence — the second call IS the conversion for that sequence
