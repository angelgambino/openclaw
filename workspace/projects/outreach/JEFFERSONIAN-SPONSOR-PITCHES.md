# Jeffersonian Dinner — Sponsor Pitch Emails

**Context:** Intimate 12-16 person dinners with founders and investors. Chatham House Rules. Single conversation format (no side chats). Designed for deep, meaningful connection — not networking events.

**Pricing:** $5,000–$10,000 per dinner (tiered based on sponsorship level)

---

## Sponsorship Package Overview

Include this in conversations, not in the cold email (save it for the follow-up):

**Standard Sponsorship ($5,000):**
- Brand presence at dinner (place cards, menu, subtle signage)
- 2-minute welcome/intro at the start of dinner
- Full attendee list with contact info (with attendee consent)
- Post-dinner email intro to any attendee you'd like to connect with
- Social media mention in event recap

**Premium Sponsorship ($10,000):**
- Everything in Standard, plus:
- Co-host credit (your team member joins as a guest)
- 5-minute thought leadership moment (not a pitch — a genuine conversation starter)
- Priority access to future dinner attendee lists
- Co-branded invite to attendees
- Dedicated follow-up email to attendees featuring your product/resource

## Cold Sponsor Outreach Template

For companies not yet in your network:

**Subject:** [Company Name] + Angel Club — your ICP, one dinner table

**Body:**

> Hey [Name],
>
> I'm Angel, founder of Angel Club — a community of founders and early-stage investors in the Bay Area.
>
> We host Jeffersonian dinners: intimate 12-16 person dinners with founders (seed to Series B) and active investors. One conversation, Chatham House Rules. No panels, no pitches, just real conversation.
>
> I'm reaching out because the founders at these dinners are [Company Name]'s ideal customers:
>
> - [Specific reason 1 — e.g., "They're all raising or recently raised, which means they're actively setting up [the infrastructure your product provides]"]
> - [Specific reason 2 — e.g., "The investors in the room regularly recommend tools to their portfolio companies"]
> - [Specific reason 3 — e.g., "They're at the stage where they're choosing their long-term [category] partner"]
>
> We offer sponsorship packages ($5,000–$10,000) that include presenting sponsor branding, attendee list access, post-dinner warm intros, and the option to put a team member in the room as a guest.
>
> If this sounds interesting, I'd love to chat for 15 minutes. Happy to share more about the attendee profile and what past sponsors have gotten out of it.
>
> — Angel
>
> P.S. — [Optional: Social proof — "Our last dinner included founders from [notable companies] and investors from [notable funds]."]

**Tips for cold outreach:**
- **Find the right person:** Look for Head of Partnerships, Startup Partnerships, Developer Advocacy, or Marketing leads
- **LinkedIn first:** A LinkedIn connection request with a short note often gets a faster response than cold email
- **Reference mutual connections:** If anyone in Angel Club works at or has a connection to the company, mention it
- **Follow up once** after 5 days, then move on. Don't chase sponsors — there are plenty of companies who'd love this
